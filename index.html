WHAT IS BAAP?

BAAP was created to inspire young people to be science and technology leaders, by engaging them in exciting mentor-based programs that build science, engineering, and technology skills, that inspire innovation, and that foster well-rounded life capabilities including self-confidence, communication, and leadership.

OUR ENGINEERING SUB-TEAMS

Four specialized divisions engineering high-altitude rocketry and autonomous payload flight systems.

AVIONICS & TELEMETRY

Designs custom 4-layer flight computer PCBs with Teensy 4.1, dual BMP388 altimeters, 9-DOF IMUs, and 915MHz LoRa telemetry radio links.

FLIGHT DYNAMICS & CAD

Utilizes OpenRocket, RasAero, and SolidWorks to engineer aerodynamic stability, tune fin flutter margins, and layup carbon fiber airframes.

PAYLOAD & RESEARCH

Engineers scientific payloads including atmospheric radiation sensors, high-altitude gas sampling canisters, and camera stabilization gimbals.

RECOVERY & OPERATIONS

Constructs dual-deployment main/drogue parachute recovery systems, electronic black-box tracking beacons, and directional Yagi antenna arrays.
